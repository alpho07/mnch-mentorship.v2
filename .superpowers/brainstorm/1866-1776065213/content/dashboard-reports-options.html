⭐ My recommendation: Option B (Unified Scrollable) with a twist

Show role badges in the header so the user always knows their context. Below that, render only the sections their roles entitle them to — an assessor-only user sees only the assessment section, a mentor+assessor sees both. A mentee sees an "My Classes" section instead. This makes the dashboard feel personal rather than generic, and every section has a clear "See all →" that deep-links into the relevant tab. No switching, no toggling — just scroll.

For an Assessor, "Reports" means assessment analytics (section averages, trends, PDF exports).
For a Mentor, "Reports" means mentorship analytics (class progress, attendance rates, mentee completion).

⭐ Recommendation: Option A (Inner Tabs) for Reports

Reports are structurally very different between domains — assessment reports have section averages, PDF exports, and email jobs; mentorship reports have cohort completion, attendance rates, and per-mentee breakdowns. Trying to merge these into one scroll or feed creates a confusing mixed bag. Inner tabs give each domain its own full-screen space while keeping a single "Reports" entry in the bottom nav. For single-role users, tabs don't appear at all — it just renders the relevant view directly.
