What should the EmONC dashboard focus on?

For a mentor or program manager opening the EmONC dashboard, what is the most important thing to see first?

A

Per-mentee completion map

A big table/grid at the top: mentees as rows, modules/tracks as columns, and activity status (CME / Hands-on / Drill) as colored chips. At a glance you see who is blocked where.

B

KPI cards + pending actions

Cards for active mentees, modules completed, pending video reviews, pending mentor approvals, pending Head DRMH certifications. Each card clicks through to a filtered list.

C

Both: KPI cards first, then completion map

Top row of KPIs and pending-action counts, followed by a scrollable completion matrix. This gives both executive summary and operational detail on one page.
