Design System Summary
Here's what will be applied across all screens when you approve:
  • Typography: -apple-system / SF Pro — same font stack as iPhone. Letter-spacing tightened to −0.4px on headings for that premium feel.
  • Colours: Hero panels deep navy → indigo (#1A1A2E → #2D3B8E). Accent #4F6AF5 on buttons, active nav, badges, progress fills. Background #F2F2F7 (iOS system grey).
  • Cards: Pure white #FFFFFF, border-radius 14–18px, ultra-subtle shadow. No coloured borders — colour lives in icons and badges only.
  • Navigation: Frosted-glass bottom bar. Active tab gets a filled indigo pill behind the icon + coloured label. Inactive icons neutral grey.
  • Animations: fadeInUp (0.4s, staggered per card), scaleIn on modals, spring cubic-bezier(0.34,1.56,0.64,1) on interactive elements.
  • Scope: constants.js token update + index.css keyframes + all screen files restyled (login, dashboard, lists, detail, forms, nav).
